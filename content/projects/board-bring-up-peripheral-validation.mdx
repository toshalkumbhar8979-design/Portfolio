## Project Overview

Performed board bring-up and peripheral driver validation on an Arduino-class microcontroller platform, with an emphasis on timing, fault recovery, and repeatable debugging.

### Bring-Up & Driver Development

*   **Board Bring-Up:** Verified power rails, clocks, and peripheral connectivity using a multimeter and logic-level checks.
*   **Low-Level Drivers:** Wrote and debugged drivers for GPIO, timers, PWM, I2C, and UART.
*   **Timing Validation:** Validated timing accuracy and interrupt latency under varying system loads.
*   **Fault Handling:** Created unit-test-style validation routines covering driver edge cases, error paths, and recovery behavior.
*   **Documentation:** Documented the bring-up sequence and known failure modes for repeatable validation.

### Skills Applied

*   **Embedded Systems:** C/C++, Arduino-class microcontrollers
*   **Peripherals:** GPIO, timers, PWM, I2C, UART
*   **Validation:** Hardware debugging, timing analysis, unit-test-style routines
