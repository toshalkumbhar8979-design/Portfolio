## Project Overview

Engineered a custom 6-DOF robotic manipulator from June 2026, driven by an Arduino Uno and PCA9685 PWM driver over I2C.

### Contributions

* Implemented a 115200-baud binary serial protocol with a 500 ms watchdog for fail-safe actuation.
* Built a FastAPI and WebSockets control system streaming at 30 Hz, with a 3D URDF digital twin and live switching between Cartesian inverse kinematics and direct joint control.
* Applied an EMA low-pass filter with S-curve cosine velocity interpolation to guarantee zero-jerk motion across all six servo axes.
* Recorded and exported 250+ teleoperated demonstration episodes through a PS5 DualSense controller for PyTorch behavior-cloning imitation learning.
* Extended the system with ROS 2, MoveIt, and Gazebo for live remote control of the physical arm through its simulated digital twin.
